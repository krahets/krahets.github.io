Yudong Jin 靳宇栋

I am a fourth-year PhD student (expected to graduate in 2027) in Computer Science at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Xiaowei Zhou and Prof. Sida Peng. I am also a research intern at Robbyant, mentored by Prof. Yinghao Xu. I received my M.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and B.Eng. from the Qianxuesen Class at Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research focuses on spatial intelligence, with a particular interest in lifting video models into 3D space to perceive our physical world. I am also passionate about open-source education and have built Hello Algo.
